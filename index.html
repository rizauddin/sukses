How stars are earned

Each completed focus session earns one star, including custom durations of 5–90 minutes. Pausing and resuming is allowed. Breaks and skipped or reset attempts earn no stars. A fresh attempt after Reset can earn a star when completed.

Four sessions make a cycle, with no daily limit.

Backup and data

No accounts, tracking or cloud sync. Browser and installed-app storage may be separate. Clearing browser data, uninstalling or changing devices may erase progress. Keep a backup.

Install and use offline

On iPhone/iPad: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Wording may vary by iOS version.

On Android: use the install button when available, or look for Install app / Add to Home screen in the browser menu. Support varies by browser.

Open once online and wait for ready status before reopening offline. Browsers may evict stored files. Sounds or alerts while the app is closed are not guaranteed.
